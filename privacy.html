Privacy Policy — Pro Trade Play (Educational Virtual Trading Simulator)
Package: com.protradeplay.app

1. Introduction
This privacy policy describes how Pro Trade Play ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the app. The app is an educational virtual trading simulator focused on Indian NIFTY and BANK NIFTY indices. No real-money trading, deposits, or withdrawals are supported. All capital, balances, rewards, and credits are virtual and have no monetary value.

2. Information we collect
Account information (Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In): Firebase user ID (UID), email address, display name, profile photo URL, and Google Sign-In profile information. Sign-in is required to watch rewarded ads and redeem rewards.

App data (Cloud Firestore), associated with your UID:
- user profile
- wallet data (virtual capital and trade credits)
- rewards and reward transactions
- trades and positions (virtual simulator records)
- trading journal entries

Advertising (Google AdMob rewarded ads): advertising ID / device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See section 5.

Usage and diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics and/or Google Analytics may collect crash logs and usage data if enabled.

3. How we use information
- To authenticate you and maintain your account
- To store and sync your virtual wallet, rewards, trades, positions, and journal across devices
- To show rewarded ads and grant virtual rewards for completed ads
- To operate, maintain, and improve the app

4. Sharing and disclosure
We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by service providers:
- Google Firebase (authentication, Cloud Firestore, crash reporting, analytics)
- Google AdMob (ad serving and measurement)

We do not share data with third parties for their own marketing purposes except as described for advertising.

5. Advertising and consent
The app uses Google AdMob rewarded ads and a user-consent platform (UMP) where required by applicable law (for example, EEA and UK). You may be presented with a consent form before ads are loaded. Ad serving respects your choices; withholding consent may disable rewarded ads. Advertising ID may be used for personalized ads where consent is granted. Rewarded ads grant virtual capital or trade credits only — no real money is paid out.

6. Data retention
Account and Firestore data is retained while your account exists. You may request account deletion (see section 8). Advertising data retention is governed by Google's policies.

7. Children
This app is an educational virtual trading simulator with no real-money transactions and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Account deletion
To request deletion of your account and associated Firestore data, email: protradeplay@gmail.com
Account deletion method: send an email to protradeplay@gmail.com from your registered Google account email with the subject "Delete my Pro Trade Play account". Deletion removes your Firebase Auth user record and associated Firestore documents (wallet, rewards, trades, positions, journal entries). Advertising identifiers are handled by Google. Requests are processed within 7 to 30 days.

9. Security
Data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS. Firestore access is protected by Firebase security rules (firestore.rules). No system is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers
Data may be processed in the United States or other countries where Google operates infrastructure.

11. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
